The intellectual foundation

The Science Behind Negotiation Architecture

Understand the forces that shape decisions, behaviour, value and agreement.

A research-informed perspective considers the economic and behavioural dimensions of negotiation together.

Negotiation is not only about what people say at the table. It is also about the deeper layers beneath the surface that shape every decision and outcome.

This perspective asks what each party values, what alternatives exist, how information is shared, where uncertainty sits and how decisions are influenced by relationships and context. The objective is to replace assumptions with inquiry and reaction with deliberate judgement.

Economics

Value and alternatives

Understand the value at stake, the available choices and the trade-offs that shape an agreement.

Behaviour

People and perception

Consider how trust, emotion, communication and cognitive judgement influence the conversation.

Relationships

Conflict and collaboration

Examine how parties move through disagreement, exchange information and build workable commitments.

Doctoral research

From discord to collaboration.

Science informs the system. Practice tests it.

Research focus

What helps people move from disagreement towards collaboration?

Doctoral research examined negotiation outcomes in UAE supply chain relationships and facilities management. It explored trust, information sharing and conflict resolution, and their relationship with supply chain performance.

The study was conducted at Clermont Business School.
